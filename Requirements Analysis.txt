Requirements Analysis

1. Home Page (index.html)

Display the StudentHub welcome page.
Provide navigation links to all other pages.

2. Login Page (login.html)
Allow users to enter username/email and password.
Provide Login and Reset buttons.

3. Dashboard (dashboard.html)
Display an overview of student information.
Provide quick access to all modules.

4. Attendance (attendance.html)
Display subject-wise attendance details.
Show attendance percentage.

5. Assignment (assignment.html)
Display assignment details and due dates.
Show submission status.

6. Study Material (material.html)
Display available study materials.
Provide links to view or download materials.

7. Timetable (timetable.html)
Display the weekly class schedule.
Show subject, time, and faculty details.

8. Grades (grades.html)
Display subject-wise marks and grades.
Show overall performance.

9. Notification (notification.html)
Display important announcements.
Show notification date and description.

10. Profile (profile.html)
Display student personal and academic details.
Show contact information and enrollment details.
